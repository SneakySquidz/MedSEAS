# MedSEAS dashboard

This repo is public: keep everything here free of personal and client details.

## Report back (every session)
When a session here changes this project's status, next step or a deadline, or finishes something, end your reply with a short block starting `→ CHIEF` (project, what changed, next step, any date) for the owner to paste into his own notes. Generic wording, no names or amounts. The owner's private setup rules, where present on the machine, say where that block goes.

## Working with the owner (2026-10-29)
- **Ask when in doubt.** If a choice is his, or a request could mean two things, ask rather than guess. Routine, reversible work goes ahead without asking.
- **Questions as a questionnaire:** a short question, then 2–4 options, the recommended one first marked "(Recommended)", each with a one-line reason. Never a bare open question when options exist.
- **Tone:** plain and direct, lead with the answer, no flattery, and say so when a plan looks wrong. Keep it friendly and encouraging, a kind word where it fits, but no persona or game phrasing.
